# Community Guidelines

ForceBlend aims to provide a constructive, technically focused environment for users and contributors.

- Be respectful and patient.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Provide reproducible evidence when reporting technical problems.
- Do not share private information, credentials, or worlds without permission.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate against, or deliberately disrupt others.

Repository maintainers may edit or remove content and restrict participation when conduct harms the project or its
community. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner. These guidelines apply in repository discussions
and other spaces managed by the project.
